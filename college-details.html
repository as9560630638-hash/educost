About IIT Delhi

Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, established in 1961 by an Act of Parliament, is one of the premier engineering and technology institutions in the country. Located on a lush 320-acre campus in Hauz Khas, South Delhi, IIT Delhi is recognised as an Institute of National Importance. The institute offers 102 undergraduate, postgraduate, and research programmes across 13 departments and 4 interdisciplinary centres.

IIT Delhi has consistently ranked among the top 2 engineering institutions in India by the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) and features prominently in global rankings — QS World University Rankings 2025 placed it at #150 globally. The institute has produced some of India's leading entrepreneurs, scientists, and corporate leaders.

The research output of IIT Delhi includes 1,500+ patents, collaborations with 120+ international universities, and an annual research grant intake exceeding ₹500 crore. The 650+ faculty members are 100% PhD holders, many of whom are fellows of national academies.

B.Tech Admission Process 2026 — IIT Delhi

1

Appear in JEE Main 2026

Register and appear for JEE Main 2026 conducted by NTA. Minimum 75% in Class 12 PCM required. Registration window: Nov 1 – Dec 4, 2025.

2

Qualify for JEE Advanced 2026

Top 2.5 lakh JEE Main qualified students are eligible for JEE Advanced, the gateway to all IITs. Exam date: May 2026 (tentative).

3

Register on JoSAA Portal

Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) manages centralized counselling for IITs, NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. Register online after results.

4

Fill College & Branch Preferences

Submit ordered preference list of Institute-Branch combinations. Use our free Rank Predictor tool to optimise your choices.

5

Seat Allotment (6 Rounds)

JoSAA conducts up to 6 rounds of seat allotment. Accept your allotted seat and pay the ₹35,000 seat acceptance fee online.

6

Reporting & Document Verification

Report to IIT Delhi for physical document verification. Carry original certificates: Class 10 & 12 Marksheets, ID proof, Category certificate (if applicable), and photographs.
